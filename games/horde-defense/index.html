Dev note

A combat prototype, not the full game

Horde Defense is a prototype of the Spheres of Influence combat system. Sword, shield, spin, slam, dodge — the melee kit, dropped onto a siege field so we can tell whether the fight feels right.

This is not Spheres of Influence. No planet, no voxels, no multiplayer. Just the horde, the gate, and whether swinging a sword at a mass of cubes is fun.

About the game

Combat feel first. Survive as long as the gate stands.

Waves escalate until you fall or the gate is breached. Click the canvas to capture the mouse. Esc pauses. First load downloads a WebAssembly engine, so give it a few seconds.
