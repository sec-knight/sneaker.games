How to play
  • Command centers sit in the four corners (v1 is 1v1 on opposite corners)
  • Grab the nearby POC for safe stars · fight the mid-ring · contest the center gem
  • Tap pals to select · drag a box to grab a bunch (workers busy themselves — not troops)
  • Resource: pick HQ or an eco POC to hire a worker (20 max per mine site) · Recruit: pick a barracks, then queue Sparks, Buddies, Chunks
  • Battle POCs: tap one to pick ★ Star or 🛡 Squad above it (lost if captured)
  • Wall tool: drag a line · Tower tool: tap to plant
  • Two fingers pan & pinch · mouse wheel zooms, right-drag pans
