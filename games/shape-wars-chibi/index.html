Dev note

Spheres, cubes, and a phone in your hand

Shape Wars - Chibi! is the current public demo: a small geometric war you can actually tap. Pips roll in as spheres. Tets stack up as cubes. The map is a handful of points of control, plus the walls and towers you throw up to keep them.

This is not the long game. No lore dump, no engine manifesto. Just whether capturing a point with cute little shapes feels fun on a phone, and whether the HUD buddies make the fight readable.

Try it. Then tell us if the war is cute enough.

About the game

Take the points. Keep the points. Look adorable doing it.

Two sides, two shapes. Pips and Tets contest a field of points of control. Hold a POC and it works for you. Lose it and the map starts leaning the other way. Walls and towers are how a cute army gets stubborn.

The prototype is built for the browser with a phone-first layout, so a session can start on the couch. Desktop still works — click instead of tap.
