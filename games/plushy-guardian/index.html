About the game

Small, readable, and just derpy enough.

Guide the Plushy Guardian through corrupted pockets of the Tiny Forest. Survive three enemy waves, recover lost stuffing, and cleanse the blighted tree waiting at the heart of each level.

The prototype is built directly for the browser, with quick sessions and phone-friendly controls so a new idea can go from conversation to something playable without becoming a six-month production.

MoveWASD, arrow keys, or left thumb
Vine spellSpace or the sparkle button
DodgeShift or the leaf button

Build Notes

August 13, 2026Adaptive audio

Every scene gets a theme

  • Expanded the procedural soundtrack into distinct game-state themes.
  • Added a sleepy, melody-rich welcome theme and a faster rock-and-roll bad-guy track for combat.
  • Added a bright synthesized-trumpet fanfare and celebration loop for successful level clears.
August 13, 2026Level 3

The Crimson Capwood

  • Added the third playable forest level with a red, spore-lit palette.
  • Made mushrooms the dominant enemy and added skittering, standard, and bruiser-like procedural variants.
  • Added the Sporeheart Tree finale and a complete three-level progression.
August 12, 2026Level 2

The Violet Canopy

  • Added a purple forest region led by bat swarms.
  • Introduced bat size, stretch, color, and movement variation without multiplying the art workload.
  • Added level-to-level victory flow and the Twilight Old Tree encounter.
August 12, 2026Enemies

The forest gets crowded

  • Added the three core enemy families: slime, bat, and mushroom.
  • Gave each family a distinct procedural movement style so simple art still reads with personality.
  • Added controlled scaling, stretching, and hue variation for lightweight enemy variety.
August 12, 2026Audio

Boops, doots, and forest noises

  • Added a light procedural background track with a cheerful starting-area feel.
  • Added varied attack, dodge, movement, hurt, and healing sound effects.
  • Kept audio behind the welcome interaction so it starts reliably on desktop and mobile.
August 12, 2026First playable

The Slime Clearing

  • Published the first playable browser build with keyboard and touch controls.
  • Added the Guardian, aiming wisp, vine spell, dodge, recoverable stuffing, three waves, and a blighted-tree finale.
  • Made aim direction persist after movement so attacks remain predictable on touch screens.
