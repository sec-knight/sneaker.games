MedTracker · Privacy · Effective September 29, 2026

PRIVACY POLICY

How the MedTracker iOS app handles information. MedTracker is a family medication, dose, refill, and health-journal tracker. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Product: MedTracker (iOS)
Developer: App Support
Contact: support@sneaker.games

Summary

  • MedTracker is designed to keep your health and medication data on your device.
  • The App’s core features do not require an account and do not send your medication, dose, journal, or photo data to our servers.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • If you buy MedTracker Pro, Apple processes the purchase; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • The current App binary does not include advertising, analytics, or crash SDKs that transmit data to third parties.

Information the App stores on your device

Depending on how you use MedTracker, the App may store locally on your iPhone:

  • Profiles / family members you add
  • Medication names, schedules, dose logs, and refill notes
  • Health journal entries and photos you attach
  • App settings, including whether MedTracker Pro is unlocked

This information stays in the App’s on-device storage until you delete the records or delete the App. We do not keep a server copy of your medication or journal data that we could retain or erase on your behalf.

Device backups you configure (such as iCloud Backup of the iPhone) are controlled by Apple and your Apple ID settings—not by a MedTracker cloud account.

On-device protection

App data is stored in MedTracker’s iOS app sandbox and is protected by the device’s lock-screen encryption when the iPhone is locked. We do not claim HIPAA or hospital-grade security beyond what iOS provides for normal on-device apps.

Notifications

Dose and refill reminders use iOS local notifications scheduled on your device. Reminder content is not sent to us.

Camera and photo library

If you grant permission, MedTracker may use the camera or photo library so you can attach images to journal entries or related records. Images are stored on device with your journal data. We do not upload them to MedTracker servers as part of normal App use.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

MedTracker does not currently read from or write to Apple Health (HealthKit).

Purchases (MedTracker Pro)

MedTracker may offer a one-time in-app purchase (“Pro”) that unlocks full features. Purchases and restores are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply to payment processing. We may learn from Apple whether you own the Pro unlock (for example, to restore purchases on the same Apple ID). We do not receive your full credit card number.

Data we do not collect (current design)

In the current design of MedTracker:

  • We do not operate a MedTracker account system for signing in.
  • We do not run MedTracker backend servers that receive your medication or health journal content.
  • We do not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that phone home by default.

For App Store privacy nutrition labels, this design is intended to support a Data Not Collected declaration for data collected by the developer through the App. If that changes (for example, if we add optional cloud sync, analytics, HealthKit, or a support SDK that transmits data), we will update this policy and the App Store nutrition labels before or when that change ships.

What you might still share with us

  • If Apple’s App Store Connect / Xcode crash reporting is enabled for the App, Apple may provide us crash reports (for example stack traces, device model, and OS version). Those reports are not your journal text or medication logs.
  • If you email support and voluntarily send screenshots or an export, we use that only to respond, keep it only as long as needed to help, then delete it. We do not add it to a marketing or analytics system.
  • Visits to the MedTracker web pages on sneaker.games are separate from the App. That site is a static marketing/privacy home; it is not used to collect medication data from the App.

Sharing and export you control

You may export, back up, or share data you choose to share (for example, exporting a file or using the iOS share sheet). When you share with another app or person, that destination’s privacy practices apply. MedTracker does not automatically sync your data to other users’ devices.

Children

MedTracker is intended for use by adults managing medications for themselves or their family. It is not directed at children under 13 as a primary audience. Do not use the App to collect information from children in a way that violates applicable law (including COPPA where it applies). Caregivers who enter a child’s medication information do so under their own responsibility; that data remains on the caregiver’s device under this on-device design.

Medical disclaimer

MedTracker is a personal organization tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a medical device. Always consult a qualified clinician for health decisions. Do not disregard professional medical advice because of information in the App.

Your choices

  • You can delete profiles, medications, journal entries, and photos inside the App.
  • You can delete the App, which removes the App’s local data from that device (subject to any device backups you keep).
  • You can revoke camera or photo permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can manage App Store purchases in your Apple ID settings; MedTracker Pro is designed as a one-time unlock, not a recurring subscription.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
