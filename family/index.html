The cast

Click a card to put them in the spotlight. The models are the real studio meshes, animation-pruned so a phone can actually load them.

Mascot · center

Sneaker

The namesake. Combat idle, backflip on request, four faces hiding in one texture. If the sisters dance, he flips. That is not a bug. That is character.

Sister · stage left

Nezuko

Sketch-mask, sharp silhouette, first on the dance floor. She will look around, wave, celebrate, and punch if you ask her to.

Sister · stage right

Midnight

Same unclothed base as Nezuko, cooler lamp. After dark still counts as a showtime.

How to play with it

This is a lab bench, not a cutscene. Drag, click, mash the toolbar, and see which sibling has opinions.

Drag to walk around the family. Scroll to move in. The camera will slide toward whoever you last clicked.

Click the model or the card. They greet you. Punch is solo on purpose — some manners are personal.
